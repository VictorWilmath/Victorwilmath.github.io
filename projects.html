Synesis

Synesis is a software solution to full body tracking for SteamVR over Open Sound Control (OSC). A python/ONXX pipeline uses a webcam and SteamVR HMD pose data to read user movements, and interpret as a pose. Data is transferred over OSC to social VR applications, primarily VRChat. This is remarkable because full body tracking solutions primarily use wearable trackers, often worn on the ankles, knees, wrists, forearms, and waist. Leading trackers retail at $150 per tracker, and budget options retail at $35 per tracker. Synesis is free and open source.

Microcontroller 3D Graphics Rendering

For my final project in CMPEH-472, I decided I wanted to try and use a Raspberry Pi Pico W to render 3D graphics, and output to a TFT over SPI. I drew a shape in a memory buffer, and pushed it in parallel over SPI. Results were rendered at ~22 Hz.

Whiskard

Whiskard was my first experiment with machine learning. I trained a neural network to recognize images of my cats using PyTorch. The network was fed a dataset of a few dozen images, and could then identify which cat was in a new image provided by the user.
